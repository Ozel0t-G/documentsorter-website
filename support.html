Before reporting a problem

Useful information

What should a problem report contain?

Name the app version, device and operating-system version, what you expected, what happened instead, and the smallest set of steps that reproduces the problem.

Where can I find app-specific privacy information?

The shared privacy page links to the complete policy for each app.

When will I receive a reply?

These are hobby projects rather than a full-time business, so replies may take a few days.
